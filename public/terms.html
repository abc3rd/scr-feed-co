Order-ahead terms & SMS information

Updated October 11, 2026

These terms describe the current SCR Feed Co. order-ahead application for Sweet Cypress Ranch. Contact staff at your SCR store for assistance with an order or these terms.

Orders, availability, and payment

Review the product, package size, quantity, and store before submitting. Prices and available quantities are checked when the order is accepted. The displayed item subtotal excludes tax and delivery fees. Payment is collected through the store at pickup or hand-off; this app does not charge a card or place a card hold.

Order status is available in the app. Customers may cancel their own Pending Ticket orders. Contact store staff for later cancellations, returns, refunds, and scheduling questions. A delivery request is not a confirmed appointment; staff must confirm availability, service area, date, and any delivery fee.

Your account

Use accurate contact information and keep your sign-in credentials private. Account deletion is available from Profile after active orders are cancelled or completed. Personal store data and the sign-in account are removed by the deletion flow; completed and cancelled order records are retained without the customer identity for store accounting. If deletion cannot finish, the app reports the issue and permits a retry.

SMS and email preferences

The current app saves contact preferences but does not send automated SMS or email campaigns. Enabling a preference is optional and is not required to place an order. You can update saved choices in Profile.

The optional SMS preference covers order updates, reorder reminders, and offers from SCR Feed Co. If a messaging program is activated, frequency will vary and message and data rates may apply. Before messages are sent, SCR must configure and verify consent handling, STOP opt-out, HELP support, sender details, and the program's privacy disclosures. Saving a preference in this app does not itself activate that program.

Ask SCR store staff for help with saved preferences. Do not send STOP or HELP to an unverified phone number; no automated messaging sender is configured by this build.

Changes and store policies

SCR should review and approve these operational terms before a customer rollout and update them when payment, delivery, or messaging features change. Store-specific return and delivery policies must be confirmed directly with SCR staff.
